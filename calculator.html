Understanding the Limitations

This calculator uses simplified category-level manufacturing-footprint assumptions. It does not measure the exact emissions of a particular device.

Actual environmental benefits depend on the repair required, replacement decisions, transport, materials, energy consumption and how long the repaired device continues to be used.

The probability of avoiding replacement is a user-selected scenario assumption, not a scientifically measured prediction about an individual.

The calculation should therefore be interpreted as an indicative scenario, not a guaranteed carbon saving.
